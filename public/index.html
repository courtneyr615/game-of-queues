Instructions and scoring

Everyone starts with 20 points and moves independently. Roll, then click a room or hallway square. Each roll reaches at least one other unsolved room where available; an announced travel bonus covers routes longer than 12. Entering a room ends that move. You may roll again immediately, or choose New move to discard unused movement.

Each player can solve each room once for 50 points. The first solver receives an additional 25 points. Clues cost 5 points per room and save to your private notes. Secret passages cost 10 points per use; scores may go below zero. A correct optional patient-identification bonus earns 50 points; skipping costs nothing. Responses without a configured patient answer are saved for review.

The game finishes when every remaining player has solved all eight mystery rooms and answered or skipped their final bonus. Pending review answers do not receive automatic bonus points. Casework, Data Entry, Identification, Supervisors, Prescription Resolution, Orders, and Communication Error have supplied patient scenarios; Desktop is a thank-you room: each player earns 25 points on their first visit, with no accusation, paid clue, or patient bonus question. AE/PC/OSF also has a supplied investigative scenario.
